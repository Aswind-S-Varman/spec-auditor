# Pricing and Discount Specification

## 1. Discount Tiers

Discounts are applied to the basket subtotal before tax.

- Orders over RM100 receive a 10% discount.
- Orders between RM50 and RM100 receive a 5% discount.
- Orders below RM50 receive no discount.

## 2. Shipping

Orders of RM150 or more qualify for free shipping. All other orders are charged
a flat shipping fee of RM8.90.

## 3. Promotional Codes

A shopper may apply one promotional code per order. Promotional discounts stack
with tier discounts.

Promotional discounts do not stack with tier discounts.

## 4. Currency

All amounts are displayed in Malaysian Ringgit with two decimal places.
